# Product Vision and Strategic Brief

Version: 0.1
Status: Draft for validation

## 1. Product vision

Build a high-end personal portfolio that presents Mayo T. N. Kadanga as a single, coherent professional identity across economics, statistics, data science, research, consulting, and software development.

The website must prove competence through selected case studies, reproducible technical work, research outputs, and polished digital execution.

## 2. Problem statement

Mayo has a broad and credible profile, but the breadth creates a communication risk.

A visitor may see multiple roles:

- Ingénieur Statisticien Économiste;
- Data Analyst;
- Data Scientist;
- econometrician;
- full-stack developer;
- consultant;
- teacher;
- researcher;
- co-founder and lead of EM² Data & AI Lab.

Without a clear narrative, this can appear fragmented. The portfolio must show that these roles are components of one practice:

> transforming complex questions into evidence, analytical systems, and digital tools for decision-making.

## 3. Product goals

### Primary goals

- Establish a clear and memorable professional positioning.
- Demonstrate depth through a small number of strong case studies.
- Support applications for data, economics, research, and analytics roles.
- Support consulting and institutional collaboration opportunities.
- Support doctoral and research applications.
- Demonstrate software engineering and product-development capability.
- Create a durable platform for future writing, teaching, and technical work.

### Secondary goals

- Strengthen search visibility for Mayo's name and expertise.
- Build a public archive of selected research and technical notes.
- Connect the personal brand to EM² Data & AI Lab without duplicating its corporate site.
- Provide reusable content for applications, proposals, social media, and presentations.

## 4. Non-goals

The first version is not intended to be:

- a social network;
- a generic personal blog;
- a complete archive of every professional task;
- a public repository for confidential institutional data;
- a full learning-management system;
- a portfolio that depends on heavy 3D interaction;
- a second version of the EM² Data & AI Lab website.

## 5. Target audiences

### Primary audience A: recruiters and hiring managers

They need to understand within one minute:

- what Mayo does;
- where he creates value;
- which problems he can solve;
- whether he has delivered comparable work;
- how to access his CV and contact him.

### Primary audience B: institutional and consulting decision-makers

They need evidence of:

- methodological rigor;
- experience with public and development-sector projects;
- ability to translate requirements into operational systems;
- ability to communicate findings;
- professional reliability.

### Secondary audience A: researchers and doctoral supervisors

They need evidence of:

- research interests;
- methodological ability;
- reproducible work;
- publications, proposals, and technical writing;
- alignment with potential research themes.

### Secondary audience B: technical peers and collaborators

They need access to:

- code;
- system architecture;
- technical notes;
- implementation choices;
- GitHub and open-source work.

### Tertiary audience: students and learners

They may use:

- tutorials;
- course notes;
- examples;
- teaching resources;
- public technical articles.

## 6. Positioning

### Recommended professional title

**Economic Data Scientist and Full-Stack Analytics Builder**

### Supporting description

I transform complex data, economic questions, and institutional needs into decision-ready models, analytical systems, and digital products.

### French equivalent

**Ingénieur Statisticien Économiste et concepteur de solutions analytiques et numériques**

Je transforme les données, les questions économiques et les besoins institutionnels en modèles, systèmes analytiques et produits numériques d'aide à la décision.

### Signature

**From evidence to decisions. From models to digital systems.**

## 7. Brand relationship with EM² Data & AI Lab

The personal portfolio and EM² must be related but distinct.

### Personal portfolio

- centered on Mayo;
- shows individual thinking, contribution, and growth;
- supports employment, consulting, research, and collaboration;
- uses a more personal editorial voice;
- may include experiments and learning notes.

### EM² website

- centered on the firm;
- presents collective capabilities;
- addresses institutional and business clients;
- uses a corporate voice;
- presents services and divisions.

The personal portfolio may reference Mayo's leadership role and the EM² website case study, but should not reproduce the firm's service catalogue.

## 8. Experience principles

1. Clarity before spectacle.
2. Evidence before claims.
3. Selected depth before exhaustive listing.
4. Institutional credibility with modern execution.
5. Interaction must demonstrate competence.
6. The site must remain useful without animation.
7. Every major claim should connect to a project, result, publication, or artifact.
8. Research depth should be available without overwhelming non-technical visitors.
9. The visual system should feel data-native, not developer-template driven.
10. Confidentiality must be protected by design.

## 9. Initial flagship case studies

1. EM² Data & AI Lab website
2. Evaluation of decentralization impact in Togo
3. Economic indicators and seasonal-adjustment workflow
4. Financial inclusion and FinTech analysis

Possible later additions:

- Ministry of Justice baseline study;
- Shiny sampling simulator;
- FinMath Lab;
- financial APIs and analytical tools;
- teaching systems and educational products.

## 10. Success definition

The product succeeds when a qualified visitor can answer the following questions quickly:

- Who is Mayo?
- What type of problems does he solve?
- What evidence proves it?
- Which project should I inspect?
- How can I contact or evaluate him further?
